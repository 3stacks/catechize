**Description (4000):**

```
Hearers helps you and your family learn the confessions of the church by heart.

"Be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only." (James 1:22)

WHAT YOU CAN LEARN
• The Westminster Shorter and Larger Catechisms and the Westminster Confession
• The Heidelberg Catechism, the Belgic Confession and the Canons of Dort
• Luther's Small and Large Catechisms
• The Apostles', Nicene, Athanasian and Chalcedonian creeds
• The Ten Commandments
• Any passage of Scripture, in the King James Version

HOW IT WORKS
Hearers asks for an answer one clause at a time. Then it shows the answer, and you say how well you knew it. Spaced repetition (FSRS) brings each answer back just before you would forget it, so a few minutes each day is enough.

• Every proof text opens in the King James Version
• "Listen" reads each answer aloud
• Daily reminders on your device
• Back up your progress to a file, or sync with your own Hearers server

PRIVATE
Hearers has no account, no advertisements and no tracking. Your answers and your progress stay on your device. The readings are in the app, so it works with no connection.

FREE
Hearers is free, and its code is open source.
```

**What's new (version 1):**

```
The first release on the App Store.
```

**App Privacy:** select "Data Not Collected". The app has no analytics and no account. Optional sync goes
only to a server that the user enters.

**Notes for the reviewer:**

```
No account is needed. Choose a tradition (Reformed or Lutheran), then open a catechism and tap a question to start. Sync in Settings is optional and needs the address of a self-hosted Hearers server; the app works fully without it. Reminders are local notifications.
```
